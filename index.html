About Our Alumni Association

125 Years of Excellence — Uniting Our Alumni Worldwide

Where every alumnus is family.

For over a century, our alumni association has been the beating heart connecting generations of graduates. From our corridors of learning to the far corners of the world, we remain bound by shared memories, shared values, and a shared commitment to excellence.

We facilitate lifelong friendships, professional networking, and give back to our beloved school — ensuring future generations inherit the same transformative experience we were privileged to receive.
